A little guide to your discovery plan

Steam-wide discovery checks a random sample of the broader Steam game catalog each run. GameScope’s criteria: released Windows games with 20–5,000 reviews and at least 85% positive feedback. It is a growing sample, not Steam’s own personalized recommendation feed.

Your selected category is required for every option. Roguelike includes sourced roguelite and action-roguelike tags; Adventure alone does not qualify. Different-style discovery excludes any game overlapping your usual resolved categories. Previously recommended games are excluded unless you check the history box. If matches run out, fewer results are shown.

Step outside my usual styles favors categories farther from your play history and avoided categories, with a preference for different categories among the alternatives. Genre distance is a transparent design rule, not proof of opposite gameplay or a particular mood. Results remain randomized and exclude previously recommended games by default.

Choose Find my next hidden gem to generate fresh results. Refreshing the browser reopens the saved plan. The first Steam-wide scan can take longer while the catalog loads.

Trial time is your planned time budget, not a prediction of game length or session suitability. Save/pause behavior is unverified. No Steam history means no ownership check.

How the paths and variety work

All options must pass your required category and avoid every explicitly excluded category. Step outside my usual styles also excludes usual Steam categories (at least half the weight of your strongest style). It needs resolved history or an explicit avoided category. Category distance uses three design families: Action/Adventure/RPG/Horror/Platformer/Roguelike; Puzzle/Strategy/Simulation/Casual; and Racing/Sports. Matching categories have similarity 1, different categories in the same family .5, and different families 0. Distance is 1 minus the strongest similarity to your weighted play styles or explicit exclusions. This is a broad heuristic, not a claim about opposite mechanics, pace, mood, or difficulty. Ranking uses 65 points for similarity or distance, 25 for approval, and 10 for review support. Discovery alternatives prefer different displayed categories when available. Random selection runs after all strict filters, without replacement. Prior recommendations stay excluded unless you explicitly include history for that request. An exhausted pool returns fewer or no matches.
